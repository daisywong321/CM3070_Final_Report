About PlayGarden

PlayGarden is a small, calm collection of ten learning games designed for children aged three to five. Every game uses big, friendly buttons, clear spoken and visual feedback, and short play loops that suit a young child's attention span.

Safe by design

There are no advertisements, no external links, no chat, and nothing to buy. Nothing your child taps can take them outside the app. No personal data is collected.

How stars work

Children earn stars for finishing rounds. Stars are simply encouragement; there is nothing to unlock or spend. You can reset them below.

Tips for playing together

Sit alongside your child for the first few rounds, name the colours, shapes and numbers aloud, and let them lead. Short sessions of 10–15 minutes work best at this age.
